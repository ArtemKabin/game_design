# 0001: Puzzle game instead of game-loop or inverse side-scroller

* Date: kickoff meeting, October 2026 (recorded 2026-10-04)
* Status: accepted
* Participants: Person A, Person B

## Context
Three genres were brainstormed:

* Game-loop games: judged too plain.
* Inverse side-scroller that trolls the player (platform disappears when you try to jump on it): judged too obvious and already too well known as a game.
* Puzzle games.

Within puzzle games two concepts came up: a top-down space puzzler and a game built on German word play.

## Decision
We make a puzzle game and combine both concepts: a top-down space puzzler whose puzzles are German word-play traps ("obvious is wrong").

## Consequences
The core mechanic is instruction text with a double meaning. Every level needs a German instruction, an obvious wrong reading, and a hidden correct one. Visual polish is secondary to the puzzle logic.
